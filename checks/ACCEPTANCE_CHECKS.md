# Acceptance checks before sharing a release

Use two ordinary accounts and a separate moderator in a staging project. These are checks to perform in your environment, not claims that a real phone or your cloud credentials were tested here.

1. **Upgrade:** existing profiles, clan selections, avatar paths and activity history remain. New accounts finish the quiz/tutorial natively or on the website. Signing out/in does not show another account’s local drafts.
2. **Recording:** allow/deny location; record walk/run/ride; pause/resume; lock/switch apps and confirm recording pauses. Force-close/reopen and recover at the saved checkpoint. No distance line bridges a GPS gap. Camera and sensor permission denial must not crash the recorder.
3. **Offline:** finish a recording offline, queue upload, reconnect with the app open, and confirm one remote activity and one reward. Retry after a lost response. Keep the local copy until verified. Safety events added while an upload finishes should stay queued until synchronized.
4. **Verification:** review recalculated distance, moving time and elapsed time. Invalid samples produce a readable explanation. Appeal with context, approve/reject as a different moderator, and check the audit/notification. Revoke a verified activity and confirm reward, goal and territory corrections.
5. **Challenges:** create a casual quest, join before recording, select it in the recorder, and observe verified progress. A pre-join activity, wrong activity type, unmatched club, GPX or simulation must not count. For ranked tests, complete three verified GPS activities across two Philippine dates and the tutorial. Check a daily cap, shared club goal, fixed-distance elapsed effort and post-review finalization. Do not spoof eligibility by moving the device clock for a real competition.
6. **Clubs:** join, leave, rejoin, close/open joining, remove a member, and transfer ownership. A member cannot remove another member. Earned contributions persist after leaving, while private club chat/feed access is revoked.
7. **Community:** share a verified photo/summary privately, to members and to a joined club. Confirm the other account can access only allowed summaries and no raw route. Check kudos, comments, editing and reports. Remove a reported item as a moderator. Validate camera capture on the rebuilt phone.
8. **Chat:** send in global/clan/club conversations. A direct invitation cannot receive messages before acceptance. Decline and block work. Repeated send with the same retry ID produces one message. Confirm removed/blocked access is denied by the server.
9. **Rewards:** buy an affordable item, retry purchase and check only one charge. Equip/unequip theme/frame/badge/bubble. Low balance denies purchase. Imported and practice activities add no tokens. Cosmetics never increase scoring.
10. **Analytics/import:** review all four charts with actual records. Import a valid timestamped GPX, reject a malformed/missing-time file, and confirm its explicit source label. Run desktop practice and confirm physical totals and competition do not change. Test calculated/AI fallback without credentials; no made-up statistics should appear.
11. **Territory:** administrator approves a real permitted area; verify valid loops, open/self-crossing loops, gaps, poor accuracy, rides and out-of-zone routes. With two eligible athletes, verify overlap changes only the intersecting area. Reverse a claim and check prior ownership is restored; start a new season without deleting history.
12. **Push:** opt in on a physical device with configured FCM/APNs, start the worker, receive an update, open Inbox, inspect tickets/receipts on the trusted backend, then disable/sign out. Confirm the old account no longer receives new device pushes. Test invalid/revoked tokens.
13. **Check-ins:** configure your chosen contact, test manual/unusual-motion prompts and timeouts, confirm “no response” is not “safe”, and verify the dialer opens only after an explicit tap. No one should be automatically contacted. Try without the optional sensors available.
14. **Release:** run the included checks, install a preview APK without Metro, test on your target Android devices, confirm the deployed HTTPS OAuth callbacks and verify health/log monitoring. No secrets should appear in source control or client bundles.

Never use a real emergency or unsafe exercise as a test scenario. Use the manual prompt and controlled application tests for check-in behavior.
